## Full Description

### 📧 Own Your Inbox
**Email Automator** is your personal email butler. It doesn't just filter spam; it treats your inbox as a task list that needs managing. By leveraging local AI, it categorizes, prioritizes, and even drafts responses for you—all without your data ever leaving your control.

### 🚀 Key Features

-   **Smart Categorization**: The AI automatically classifies incoming emails into Categories (Newsletter, Transactional, Social, etc.) and Priorities (High, Medium, Low) based on visual headers and content analysis.
-   **Inbox Zero Engine**: Define automation rules like "Archive Newsletters after 7 days" or "Trash Spam immediately." The engine works tirelessly in the background to keep your inbox clean.
-   **Context-Aware Agent**: Talk to your inbox. Use the **Agent** to review drafts via voice ("Read my drafts", "Send this one"), ask for configuration help, or get explanations for complex rules.
-   **Winning Responses**: For important emails, the Auto-Pilot generates "Winning Response" drafts—pre-written replies that capture your tone and intent, ready for your approval.
-   **Live Terminal**: Watch the AI think. The built-in Live Terminal shows you exactly what the Agent is analyzing, deciding, and doing in real-time.

### 🛡️ Privacy-First: Your Data, Your Infrastructure
Built on the **"Own Your Data"** philosophy.
-   **Zero Cloud Costs**: Runs entirely on your own free-tier Supabase instance and local machine.
-   **Local AI**: Uses the **RealTimeX SDK** to tap into your local models (or API keys) securely. No vendor lock-in.

### 🔌 Seamless Integration
Designed to run alongside **RealTimeX Desktop**. It detects your local AI providers automatically and syncs via the RealTimeX ecosystem.
